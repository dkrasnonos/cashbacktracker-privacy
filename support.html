How to report a problem

Include your phone model, operating system version, app version, what you were trying to do and what happened. If possible, describe the steps to reproduce the issue. Hide balances, transactions and other personal information in screenshots. Do not send card numbers, passwords or backup files.

Where is my financial data stored?

Cashback Tracker works offline without an account. Your financial records are stored on your phone. The developer has no server copy and cannot recover your records remotely.

Backups and switching phones

Create a backup in the app before uninstalling it or changing phones. Keep the file in a safe location and use the app’s restore option on the new device. Backup files contain readable financial records and are not encrypted by the app. If you choose cloud storage through the system file picker, that provider handles file transfer and storage.

Shared budget and Bluetooth

Keep both phones nearby, turn on Bluetooth and allow the requested Bluetooth or nearby-device permissions. Open the Bluetooth synchronization screen on both phones. On the first connection, check that the same verification code appears on both devices. If an exchange does not complete in the background, unlock both phones, open the app and try again. Shared-account data is exchanged directly between devices; internet access is not required.

Cashback amounts

Recommendations depend on the cashback rules you enter. Check each card’s categories, rates and limits against your bank’s current terms. Cashback Tracker does not connect to your bank account.
